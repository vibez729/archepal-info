ArchePal Update Publisher

Privacy policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This policy covers ArchePal Update Publisher's use of Google services and this informational website. The publisher is operated by the ArchePal maintainer, vibez729.

Google information we access

The publisher requests Google's limited drive.file permission. This allows access to files and folders created by the publisher or explicitly selected for it, including update-file contents and metadata such as file identifiers and names.

This permission does not grant unrestricted access to the account's other Drive files, Gmail messages or contacts. Authorization is intended for the publisher account; recipients do not need to authorize Google access to download updates.

How we use that information

Google access is used only to create, manage and distribute application updates. Files intended for distribution are made available to recipients through Google Drive sharing. Google account information is not used for advertising, profiling or unrelated purposes.

Storage and retention

Publisher authorization credentials are kept in private local storage. They are not published on this website or distributed with the application. Update files and associated metadata are retained while needed for distribution, support and recovery.

This website has no sign-in form and does not request or store visitors' Google credentials.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell Google user data or transfer publisher authorization credentials to recipients. Google hosts the update files; GitHub hosts this website and the support page. These providers process information under their own policies: Google Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Website visits and support

We have not added analytics, advertising trackers or contact forms to this website. Hosting-provider processing remains subject to the provider's policy. If you post a support request on GitHub, its contents may be public; avoid including credentials or sensitive personal information.

Your choices

The authorizing account holder can revoke publisher access through Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future authorized publisher operations. For privacy questions or a request concerning information held by the publisher, use our support page; we can arrange an appropriate private channel if needed.

Changes to this policy

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.
